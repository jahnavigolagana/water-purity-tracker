Frequently Asked Questions

The purity score uses a weighted mathematical algorithm evaluating deviation from WHO and Indian BIS (IS 10500) drinking standards: pH (30%), TDS (30%), Turbidity (25%), and Temperature (15%). Water is rated SAFE when the score is ≥ 75 and no critical threshold is breached.

  • pH Level: 6.5 to 8.5
  • TDS (Total Dissolved Solids): ≤ 500 ppm
  • Turbidity: ≤ 5.0 NTU
  • Temperature: 20.0°C to 35.0°C

The backend module rules.py evaluates entered parameters against chemical rule conditions. For example: High TDS (>500 ppm) triggers Reverse Osmosis (RO); Low pH (<6.5) triggers Calcite Neutralization; High Turbidity (>5 NTU) triggers Sand Filtration & UV Disinfection.

All readings are automatically saved directly into data/water_readings.csv inside the backend folder. You can export or download the raw CSV anytime from the Dashboard or Reports section.
